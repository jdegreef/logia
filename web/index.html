The Word, and what the church has said about it.

Logia sets each verse of Scripture beside two thousand years of commentary: the early church, the Middle Ages, the Reformers, the Puritans and the 1800s. Every passage is copied word for word from a public-domain edition, so it is free to read and free to share.

How Logia works

Copied, never rewritten

Each excerpt is checked against the source it came from, and links to the full text. Where a page scan misread a letter, the correction is listed in the open data.

On a modern translation

The Bible text is the Open Word Bible, translated from Hebrew and Greek with the reasoning behind every sentence. Tap a word for its Greek.

English first, then many languages

Logia starts in English. Spanish and Portuguese come next, then French, Swahili, Amharic, Korean and Vietnamese, each reviewed by native speakers.
